Questions

The honest answers

Is the villa booked yet?

Not yet, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Rooms are being held first so we book a house that fits the number of people actually coming. That is why no money is taken on this page. Once the house is confirmed you get the address, the room and the payment details.

What if it does not fill?

Then it does not run and you pay nothing. Nothing is taken up front, so there is nothing to refund.

Do I have to have been at the August pop up?

No. Most people will have been, and that is the easiest way in, but if you are building something and you can hold your own in a house full of people doing the same, that is enough.

Where exactly in Da Nang?

The final villa is being locked once the numbers are in. It will be in Da Nang, walking distance to the beach, with a private room and bathroom for everyone. You get the exact address before any money moves.

How fast is the wifi?

We will publish the real number once we have run a speed test in the house, and not before. Anyone quoting you a wifi speed for a villa they have not stood in is guessing.

Can I come for two weeks instead of a month?

Ask. The room is priced for the month and a full month is the point, but if half a month is the difference between coming and not, say so in the form.

What about the weather?

September is the front edge of the rainy season here. Expect real rain. This is a month for working indoors with a pool outside, and the price reflects the season.

Can I bring a partner?

Yes, if they are fine sharing your room and being around a house that is working. Put it in the form so we count the head.
